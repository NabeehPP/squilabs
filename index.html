The process

01

Intro call

30 minutes to understand your stage, your timeline, and the outcome the design needs to drive.

02

Scope & sprint plan

A fixed quote and a week-by-week plan before anything is billed. No hourly surprises.

03

Design sprints

We work in your Slack, sharing drafts every 2–3 days instead of a big reveal at the end.

04

Handoff

Source files, a usage guideline, and a walkthrough so your team can extend the work.

Built for the calendar you have.

Speed over polish

A good deck two weeks before your board meeting beats a perfect one after it.

Clarity over cleverness

If a slide needs a paragraph to explain, it's not done. We push toward the plainest truth.

Ownership over hours

You'll never see a line-item timesheet. We quote the outcome, not the hours to get there.

Direct access

You work with the same four or five people from kickoff to handoff — no rotating account team.
