Our story

Started in a
co-working closet.

SquiLabs began in 2019 when two designers kept getting asked by founder friends to "just fix the deck real quick." Six years later we're a ten-person studio that still says yes to that request — we've just gotten a lot better at everything around it.

2019

Two freelancers, one shared inbox

Founders Priya Chandran and Tomas Reyes start taking on deck and brand work between their own product jobs.

2021

First full-time hires

SquiLabs becomes a studio in name and practice, bringing on a product designer and a copy lead to round out the team.

2023

100+ rounds supported

Client decks cross the $100M raised mark. The studio settles into the sprint-based model it still uses today.

2026

Ten people, three disciplines

Investor storytelling, brand identity, and product design operate as one team instead of three separate offerings.

How we work

Values that show up in the work

Speed over polish

Ship the 80%, fast

A good deck two weeks before your board meeting beats a perfect one after it. We optimize for the calendar you actually have.

Clarity over cleverness

Say the hard thing simply

If a slide needs a paragraph to explain, it's not done. We push every project toward the plainest version of the truth.

Ownership over hours

Fixed price, fixed scope

You'll never see a line-item timesheet. We quote the outcome, not the hours it takes to get there.
