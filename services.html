How it runs

The process

STEP 01

Intro call

30 minutes to understand your stage, your timeline, and the outcome the design needs to drive — usually a raise or a launch.

STEP 02

Scope & sprint plan

You get a fixed quote and a week-by-week plan before anything is billed. No hourly surprises.

STEP 03

Design sprints

We work in your Slack, sharing drafts every 2–3 days instead of disappearing for a big reveal at the end.

STEP 04

Handoff

Source files, a usage guideline, and a 60-minute walkthrough so your team can extend the work on its own.
